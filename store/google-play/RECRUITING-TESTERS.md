# DataLux closed-test recruiting plan

Status: preparation only. The closed track is not yet ready; do not imply an opt-in link exists.

## Who to recruit

Aim for 16–20 committed people so a few absences do not leave the test below Google's minimum.
Prioritize people who would use an offline file-transfer tool: friends and colleagues, local
makerspaces or hobby groups, educators, field workers, and privacy-minded Android users. Ask
permission before posting in a community and follow its rules. Aim for a mix of phone models,
Android versions and technical comfort levels, including people who have never seen DataLux.

The current signed candidate supports Android 13 (API 33) or newer on arm64. Each person also
needs access to a compatible second device with a display or camera for a meaningful optical
transfer. Confirm the closed track's actual country availability before promising access.

## Invitation template — send only once the track is ready

> I'm looking for volunteers to try DataLux, an early Android app that transfers files between
> devices using a screen and camera. You'll need an Android phone and access to another compatible
> device. The test runs for at least two weeks; I'd ask you to stay opted in, try a few small
> test files, and share honest feedback privately. The app is still experimental, so please use
> disposable files. You don't need to buy anything to participate. Interested? I can share the
> opt-in instructions when the test is ready.

Personalize time expectations and the feedback channel before sending. Do not promise payment,
features, compatibility, a launch date, or purchase-test access that has not been confirmed.

## Enrollment and follow-through

1. Collect the Google Play account email privately from each consenting participant, plus phone
   model and Android version. Use the minimum information required; do not publish the list.
2. After the closed release is actually available, add the list or group in Play Console and send
   each person the real opt-in link and the [quick start](TESTER-QUICK-START.md). A person only
   counts after opting in; adding an address or installing an APK does not start the requirement.
3. Verify each person remains opted in. Google's requirement for this personal account is at least
   12 opted in continuously for the preceding 14 days when applying for production access. Internal
   testers must opt out of the internal track before joining closed testing. Use the Console's
   current eligibility status; opt-out can interrupt the continuity requirement.
4. In the first days, ask each tester to install, start the app, send and receive a small synthetic
   file, and report their experience. Repeat a few scenarios after an update. Ask for actionable,
   honest feedback; do not ask people to post a positive review or merely remain inactive.
5. Track opt-in status, device, version, attempts and issues privately. Thank participants and
   communicate fixes. Continue testing if major problems persist or Google requests more testing.

Track membership does not make in-app purchases free. Only separately configured license testers
using approved test payment methods should test billing; confirm Google's test-purchase notice.
Do not request or share passwords, receipt tokens, private files or sensitive diagnostic frames.

References: [Google's testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465)
and [test-track setup](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334).
